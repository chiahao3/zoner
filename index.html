What is drawn

  • Diffraction: reflections at their excitation error sg (beam along −z). Kinematic, per-reflection two-beam (default), or many-beam Bloch wave computed when you stop moving. Convergent mode evaluates the rocking curve across every disk, so HOLZ lines and K-M fringes appear. Kikuchi bands are drawn for the strongest planes; the arrow points to the nearest zone-axis centre.
  • Depth sum / MEP slice: phase σ∫V dz by Fourier synthesis of the perfect-crystal potential, each reflection weighted by t·sinc(t gz) (slice: Δz·sinc(Δz gz) and a Gaussian depth PSF) and a Gaussian lateral PSF. Tilt smears columns by t·tanθ.
  • Resolution stereogram: centred on the beam. Colour = finest 2D lattice still resolved if you tilted there: two non-collinear reflections with |g| ≤ 1/(lateral resolution) whose depth transfer stays ≥ 0.5. Grey = only 1D fringes. Hatched = beyond the holder limits.
  • Real space: the specimen slab with slice boundaries and the current MEP slice highlighted, or a unit-cell cluster.

Qualitative tool: feature positions and symmetry are right; intensities are approximate (no absorption, perfect crystal).
