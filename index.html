What is drawn

Left column: diffraction space. Right column: real space. The orientation panel (ⓘ) sits on the right.

  • Diffraction: reflections at their excitation error sg (beam along −z). Kinematic, per-reflection two-beam (default), or many-beam Bloch wave computed when you stop moving. One convergence angle α sets the illumination: α = 0 is a parallel beam; otherwise disks of radius α are drawn as CBED once they are bigger than the spot size (zoom in to see small disks), with the rocking curve evaluated across every disk, so HOLZ lines and K-M fringes appear. Kikuchi bands are drawn for the strongest planes; the arrow points to the nearest zone-axis centre.
  • Resolution stereogram: centred on the beam. Colour = projected 2D resolution of the image currently shown (depth sum or MEP slice) if you tilted there: the worst in-plane direction still resolved by zone reflections with |g| ≤ 1/(lateral resolution) inside the first lobe of the depth transfer. Scale from the lateral resolution to 1.5 Å; grey = worse than 1.5 Å (not atomic resolution). Hover for the tilt from the beam and the α/β needed; Rings draws dashed rings of total tilt. Hatched = beyond the holder range (when the range is on).
  • Crystal model: the specimen slab with slice boundaries and the current MEP slice highlighted (atoms outside it are dimmed), or a unit-cell cluster. The legend lists each species.
  • Real-space image: depth sum (default) or MEP slice, switched in the panel header; the stereogram follows the same choice. Phase σ∫V dz by Fourier synthesis of the perfect-crystal potential, each reflection weighted by t·sinc(t gz) (slice: Δz·sinc(Δz gz) and a Gaussian depth PSF) and a Gaussian lateral PSF. Tilt smears columns by t·tanθ.
  • Holder range (switch in the orientation panel, off by default): tilt freely until the pattern matches what you see, put in the stage α/β reading, then switch on (or press L) at any time. If the current tilt is outside the range and you type nothing, it is taken as stage 0, 0. The orientation is kept, α/β now read like the stage, and tilts are limited. Limits are symmetric: 35 means −35° … +35°.

Offline: download zoner.html, a single self-contained file that runs from disk with no network (for microscope PCs).

Qualitative tool: feature positions and symmetry are right; intensities are approximate (no absorption, perfect crystal).
